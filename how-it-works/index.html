How it works

From an idea to
something wearable.

Lioo reduces the journey to three clear steps: describe, discover, and decide.

STEP 01

Describe or upload

Write what you need in natural language, or begin with a photo of a look you like.

STEP 02

Let Lioo interpret

Lioo considers the product type, style, color, occasion, budget, and other useful context.

STEP 03

Explore the result

Review relevant products or a coordinated outfit and continue the conversation to refine it.

Try saying

No special search syntax required.

Write naturally and include what matters to you.

“I need a smart-casual outfit for dinner in Sofia.”
“Find black leather boots under €150.”
“Show me items similar to this photo.”
